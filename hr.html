HR Analytics Dashboard (Using Power BI)

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive view of employee attrition and workforce trends. The dashboard analyzes employee demographics, education, salary, job roles, and years of service to identify factors contributing to attrition.




The analysis revealed an overall attrition rate of 16.1%. Employees aged 26–35 years showed the highest attrition while Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives were among the most affected roles. The dashboard enables HR teams to identify retention risks and support data-driven employee management strategies.
